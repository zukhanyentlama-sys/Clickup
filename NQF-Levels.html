Understanding qualifications

The NQF, one rung at a time

South Africa's National Qualifications Framework runs from Senior Certificate through to a doctorate. Here's what sits at each level, so you know exactly what a candidate's qualification means.

Completed Grade 12 — the National Senior Certificate. The entry point for most further study and many entry-level roles.

Why the NQF matters when hiring

It's a common yardstick. An NQF level tells you where a qualification sits relative to any other, regardless of which institution issued it — useful when comparing candidates across colleges and universities.

Level isn't the whole story

Two candidates at the same NQF level can have very different experience. Use the level as a baseline for the qualification itself, and look at years of experience and portfolio work separately.
